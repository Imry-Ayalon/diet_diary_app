אין חשבון, אין פרסומות ואין מעקב. מחיקת האפליקציה מוחקת את היומן מהטלפון.

## English

Name:
Meal Diary

Subtitle:
Meals and workouts on iPhone

Promotional text:
The diary stays on your iPhone. There is no account, and the developer does not receive your meals, photos, or reports.

Description:
Meal Diary is a private diary for meals and workouts. Entries, photos, and reports stay in the device’s memory. The developer cannot see them, and they are not sent to the developer.

Add a meal with a name, notes, and a photo, and record a workout too. Double-tap an entry to copy it onto today. After a photo, a name is suggested on the device. The photo is not sent to a server.

The app includes a daily report. You can make a PDF of the meal schedule, one page per week, and share it. The file leaves the iPhone only when you tap share, and it goes to the app you choose, not to the developer.

There is no account, no advertising, and no tracking. Deleting the app deletes the diary from the iPhone.

Keywords:
calories,nutrition,food,menu,workout,private,recipes,report

What’s new:
First release. A meal and workout diary that stays on the iPhone, with a report you can share as a PDF.
