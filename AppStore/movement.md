## English

Name:
movement

Subtitle:
Runs and rides on iPhone

Promotional text:
The route stays on your iPhone. There is no account, and the developer does not receive your location, route, or image.

Description:
movement records a run or a bike ride on the iPhone. Distance, time, ascent, speed, and the route stay in the device’s memory. The developer does not receive them.

Recording pauses by itself at a short stop, such as a traffic light, and continues when you move again. You can also pause by hand, delete a recording, or finish it. At the end you can share an image of the route with distance, time, ascent, and speed. The image leaves the iPhone only if you tap share, and it goes to the app you choose, not to the developer.

Location is used only during a run or ride, including while the screen is off, so the recording can continue. It stays on the device. To draw the map, the iPhone asks Apple for the map image of that area. That request goes to Apple, not to the developer.

Weight, if you enter it, is used only for an on-device calorie estimate. It is an estimate, not medical advice.

There is no account, no advertising, and no tracking.

Keywords:
run,ride,bike,route,cycling,pace,ascent,gps

What’s new:
First release. Run and ride recording that stays on the iPhone, with a route image you can share.

## Review notes

movement records a run or bike ride with on-device GPS. There is no account and no developer server. Location is requested only after the reviewer taps ריצה or רכיבה, and it is kept on the device so distance and the route can be recorded while the screen is locked. Please allow location. A short stop pauses the recording; moving again resumes it. Finish saves the activity. Share is optional and creates an image through the system share sheet. Map images are requested from Apple to draw the route. They are not sent to the developer. The interface is Hebrew, right to left. No sign-in is required. The calorie figure is an estimate, not a medical claim.
