App Privacy for both apps: Data Not Collected.
The developer does not collect data. Meals, photos, weight, routes, and location stay on the device. A weekly meal PDF or a ride/run image leaves the device only when the user taps share, and it goes to the app the user picks, not to the developer. movement asks Apple for a map image in order to draw a route. That request goes to Apple, not to the developer. No tracking, no advertising, no analytics.

Screenshots still have to be captured from the apps on an iPhone. App Store Connect will show the required sizes. Useful screens are the day list, a meal with a photo, the report chooser, movement’s ride and run buttons, and a saved route.
